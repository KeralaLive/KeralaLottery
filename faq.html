Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

1. Kerala Lottery Result ethra manikku varum?

Daily result uchakku 3 PM-nu draw nadakkum. Result 3:30 PM - 4 PM ullil ivide update cheyyum.

2. Innathe result evide kaanam?

Homepage-il mukalil thanne "Today Result" section-il kaanam. Full result PDF-um ivide kittum.

3. Ithile result official aano?

Yes. Kerala Government Gazette / Official result adisthanam aanu ivide kodukkunnathu. Prize vaangan ticket-um ID-um ayi official office-il verifiy cheyyuka.

4. Prize ethra divasathinullil vaanganam?

Result vannu 30 divasathinullil prize claim cheyyanam.

5. Yesterday result evide?

Homepage-il "Recent Results" section-il innale-yudeyum, munp ulla resultukalum kaanam.
